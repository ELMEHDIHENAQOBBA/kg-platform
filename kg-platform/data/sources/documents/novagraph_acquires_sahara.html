NovaGraph acquires Sahara Analytics

NovaGraph acquired Sahara Analytics in 2025, according to a statement published in Paris. NovaGraph SAS is headquartered in Paris. Sophie Laurent founded NovaGraph in 2019. Sophie Laurent is the CEO of NovaGraph SAS.

NovaGraph develops GraphPulse, a graph analytics product. NovaGraph uses Neo4j to store customer knowledge graphs. Marc Dubois is the CTO of NovaGraph. Cedar Capital invested in NovaGraph in 2022. Orion Cloud partnered with NovaGraph in 2024 to host GraphPulse.

Youssef Idrissi founded Sahara Analytics in 2020. Youssef Idrissi was the CEO of Sahara Analytics until the deal. Sahara Analytics SARL is headquartered in Rabat. Sahara Analytics develops DataLens. Sahara Analytics uses Apache Spark for batch processing. Nadia Chraibi works for Sahara Analytics.
